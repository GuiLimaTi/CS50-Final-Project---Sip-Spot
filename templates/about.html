Find the right coffee place for the right moment.

Sip Spot helps people discover cafés in Temple Bar based on what actually matters: coffee type, price, work-friendly atmosphere, Irish coffee availability, and opening hours.

Why Sip Spot exists

In Temple Bar, not every café fits the same moment. Sometimes you need a quick takeaway, sometimes a place to sit, sometimes a café that works well for studying or working, and sometimes somewhere that stays open later.

✓

What users can filter

  • Coffee type
  • Maximum price
  • Good for working
  • Sit-in or takeaway
  • Irish coffee availability
  • Opening and closing times
